# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Do not publish another person’s credentials, private infrastructure, personal data, or confidential research artifacts.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and disclosure of private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact method listed on the repository owner’s GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue when doing so would expose the affected person.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review comments, and community spaces representing the project.
